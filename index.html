LEVEL 0 · TRAINING MISSION

Meridian House, an office block. Held by the reds.

Objective — retrieve a file from a computer on their private network. It is kept in a reinforced room, and the room is heavily guarded.

Getting in — the front doors, the loading bay round the back, or the fire escape up the side. How you do it is up to you.

Getting out — back to the van, with the file.

This is a training mission: hints will appear as you go. The guards see, hear, come looking — and shoot. Stay out of sight, or stay behind something.

HOW TO MOVE

  • Vault — press Space at a low wall or railing (up to 1.25 m). Sprinting at anything waist-high takes you over or onto it without breaking stride — run up a stack of crates. Settings can raise this to 2 m, or turn it off.
  • Climb — face a ledge up to about 2.35 m and press Space. Jump and hold forward to catch one a little higher.
  • Parkour — sprint at a wall and press Space. A thin wall you go over and drop down the far side; anything taller you run a step up and haul yourself onto, up to about 3.3 m. Try the parkour lane beside the vault lane.
  • Doors — E opens and shuts an ordinary door. It swings away from you.
  • Being seen — guards see what is in front of them and hear what is loud. An arc round your crosshair shows who is noticing you: amber while they wonder, red once they have seen you. Crouching, keeping still, staying out of sight and a silencer all help. A guard who hears something goes to look where you were.
  • Takedowns — creep up behind a guard who has not seen you (crouched, he will not hear you) and press F when prompted: bare hands knock him out, a knife kills. A flashbanged guard can be taken down from any side.
  • Flashbangs and smoke — a flashbang blinds any guard with a clear line to it for a few seconds (longer the nearer, and if he was looking at it): he cannot see you or shoot, and hears little. Guards cannot see through smoke.
  • Stances — C goes from standing to crouched, again to prone, again back to standing. Holding C and letting go is just a crouch. You can't change into a taller stance without room for it.
  • Prone — very slow, almost silent, fits through the crawl pipe. Shift gives a quick, tiring shuffle. Space stands you back up.
  • Crouch-sprint — Shift while crouched: quicker than creeping, much quieter than a full sprint.
  • Dash — tap Shift while stepping left or right for a quick sideways burst. Costs stamina, with a moment before you can do it again.
  • Short jump — press C in mid-air to cut a jump short. Space from a crouch stands you up rather than jumping.
  • Ladders — walk into one facing the wall (or back onto one from a platform). W climbs, S descends. Space steps off onto a platform beside you; C lets go.
  • Falling — about 3 m is safe; 6 m hurts; 12 m nearly kills. Hold C as you land to soften it. Deep water catches you.
  • Swimming — you float at the surface. Hold C or look down and swim forward to dive; Space rises. Watch your breath. Swim to an edge and press Space to climb out.
  • Sprint — Shift, while stamina lasts. Run it dry and you'll need a moment.
  • What you carry — one primary, one secondary, two utility slots and one piece of gear. Slots go by size, not by type: two sidearms, or two lots of C5, are perfectly legal.
  • Crates — press E at a crate to swap anything, or to fit mods (optics, silencers, grips, magazines, and what's in your smoke). Whatever you swap out stays in the crate.
  • Running dry — pulling the trigger on an empty weapon starts a reload by itself. It still takes time, so switching to your secondary is often quicker.
  • Weapons — 1 to 4 to choose, click to use, R to reload. The rifle fires while you hold the button; everything else is one press at a time.
  • Peek over cover — crouched behind a crate or low wall, hold Alt and you lift your head and weapon over it to shoot, without standing up. Let go and you drop back down. It won't work where there's something directly overhead.
  • Aiming — hold Alt (or right-click) for a much tighter group. Moving and firing fast both spread your shots; the sniper's scope magnifies.
  • Grenades — press 5 for frags, 5 again for flashbangs. Mind the blast: it can reach you, though cover will save you. Looking away from a flashbang helps.
  • The range — targets at 10, 20 and 35 m, worth 10, 25 and 50. They drop when hit and pop back up.
