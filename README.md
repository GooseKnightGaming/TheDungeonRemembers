# The Dungeon Remembers

*A game that knows you're playing.*

A browser roguelike by GooseKnight. The dungeon keeps records of everything: of your
deaths, your habits, the things you rely on. And it uses them.

**▶ Play now:** https://gooseknightgaming.github.io/TheDungeonRemembers/

Runs in any modern browser, desktop or mobile. No install, no account.
Your progress is saved in your browser.
