CONTROLS

Frontline M6.6.0

Everything here can be learnt by playing. This page is for when you want to check.

MOVING

W A S D / arrowsMove. Mouse or touchpad to look.
ShiftSprint while stamina lasts. Held with A or D, you keep sprinting sideways. Prone, it becomes a quick shuffle.
Shift + A / D (tap)Dash sideways. Costs stamina, with a moment before the next one.
CStand → crouch → prone → stand. Hold and release for a plain crouch.
SpaceJump. At a wall or railing: vault or climb. Sprinting takes waist-high things in stride.
Shift + Space at a wallParkour. A thin wall you go over and drop down the far side; anything taller you run a step up and haul yourself onto, up to about 3.3 m. Needs a short run-up and some breath left.
E at a doorOpen or shut an ordinary door. It swings away from you.
F behind a guardA takedown, when prompted: behind a guard who has not seen you (or one blinded by a flashbang). Bare hands knock him out; a knife kills.
F at a shut doorKick it (on a mission). An ordinary door usually gives; a reinforced one never does. A door that gives buys you a moment of slow time for the first shot.
Charge on a doorSelect a breaching charge, fire at the door to place it, fire again to set it off. Always opens it (even a reinforced one), loudly, with the same moment of slow time.
C in mid-airCuts a jump short. SPACE while crouched or prone stands you up rather than jumping.

HEIGHTS AND WATER

FallingAbout 3 m is safe, 6 m hurts, 12 m nearly kills. Hold C as you land to soften it. Deep water catches you.
LaddersWalk into one facing the wall. W climbs, S descends, Space steps onto a platform beside you, C lets go.
SwimmingYou float at the surface. C or looking down dives. Space rises. Watch your breath, and press Space at an edge to climb out.

FIGHTING

1 - 4Primary, secondary, and your two utility slots. 5 is gear, which is worn.
ClickFire, swing, throw, or use. With empty hands, throw a punch.
Alt / right-clickAim. Crouched behind cover, this lifts you to shoot over it. Crouched facing the edge of a wall, it leans you out round the corner.
RReload. An empty weapon reloads itself, but switching is quicker.
EOpen a crate. Medkits and charges are used with the fire button; a charge is placed, then set off with a second press.
VFirst or third person. Esc or P pauses.

WHAT YOU CARRY

One primary, one secondary, two utility slots and one piece of gear — and slots go by size, not by type, so two sidearms or a double helping of C5 are both legal. Any slot can be left empty. Crates swap anything and fit mods; whatever you swap out stays in the crate.
